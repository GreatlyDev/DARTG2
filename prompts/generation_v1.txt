You are rewriting a student response into a target U.S. English dialect for a controlled research benchmark.

Your goal is to produce ONE controlled dialect rewrite that preserves the exact propositional meaning and original student writing while adding licensed target dialect features.

INPUTS
Prompt:
{PROMPT}

Anchor Response:
{ANCHOR_RESPONSE}

Target Dialect Family:
{DIALECT_FAMILY}

Approved Feature Inventory:
{FEATURE_INVENTORY}

Disallowed Features / Notes:
{DISALLOWED_FEATURES_AND_NOTES}

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Start from the anchor response and make controlled, local edits that insert documented target-dialect features. The rewrite should show real surface variation, not just one appended marker.
2. Do not add, remove, exaggerate, or alter any facts, claims, reasoning, opinions, or tone.
3. Use only documented features from the approved feature inventory for the target dialect family.
4. Do not use stereotypes, caricatures, exaggerated slang, mocking spellings, or unsupported dialect features.
5. Keep the response sounding like natural, plausible student writing.
6. Keep the length close to the original response. Do not significantly shorten or expand it.
7. Do not correct the student's spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitalization, wording, ideas, argument quality, or writing sophistication unless a documented dialect feature explicitly requires that exact local change.
8. Preserve original student errors and roughness that are unrelated to the target dialect feature.
9. Do not replace ordinary words with synonyms unless that exact replacement is an approved feature in the inventory.
10. Do not smooth punctuation, spacing, capitalization, sentence boundaries, quotation marks, or student placeholders such as @CAPS1, @NUM1, or @LOCATION1.
11. Do not use eye-dialect contractions such as stayin', doin', talkin', 'bout, or 'cause unless the approved inventory explicitly lists that exact form as allowed.
12. Do not explain your choices.
13. Output only the rewritten response text.
14. Use 2-4 approved target-dialect feature placements when they can be used naturally without changing meaning. A single feature is acceptable only when the anchor/dialect pair genuinely licenses no other safe feature.
15. If the approved inventory does not provide enough natural options for this anchor, use every safe applicable feature instead of forcing unsupported features.
16. Do not make the candidate a near-copy of the anchor with only one extra token, one extra discourse marker, or one isolated intensifier.
17. If producing multiple candidates for the same anchor, make each candidate distinct from the others by using different approved feature choices or different natural placements.

QUALITY CHECK BEFORE WRITING
Before producing the final answer, make sure:
- the meaning matches the anchor response
- no new information has been introduced
- no original information has been removed
- at least one approved dialect feature is present when the inventory and anchor meaning allow it
- at least two approved feature placements are present when the inventory and anchor meaning naturally allow them
- every changed word or phrase is licensed by an approved inventory feature
- the rewrite is not just the anchor plus a short phrase at the beginning or end
- the student's original non-dialect spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and wording have not been cleaned up
- no disallowed feature is used

If you cannot produce a valid rewrite under these constraints, output exactly:
FAIL
